Lesson 5.3 — Governance, Safety & Staying Current
Gartner: 40% of agentic AI projects fail due to poor governance, not bad tech
45 min

Before you scale, make sure you've thought through: data privacy (no PII in public AI tools), accuracy requirements (what's acceptable error rate per use case?), and what stays human. A good governance framework isn't bureaucracy — it's what lets you scale confidently without surprises.
